# Pearlz Home Systems - Troubleshooting FAQs
Q: Why is my device flashing a red LED indicator?
A: A red flashing indicator typically signifies that the primary filter cartridge has reached its lifespan capacity and requires replacement, or water flow is restricted.
Q: How do I perform a factory reset?
A: Press and hold the power button for 10 seconds until the status light blinks blue three times.
