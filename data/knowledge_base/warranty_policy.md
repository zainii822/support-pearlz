# Pearlz Home Systems - Warranty Policy

## 1. Coverage Period
The standard warranty for the AquaPearl 500 Pro purifier covers the purifier body, pump, and internal electronics for 24 months from the invoice date of purchase.

## 2. Exclusions
Filter cartridges (such as the sediment pre-filter) are classified as consumables and are explicitly excluded from the standard warranty. Physical damage, unauthorized repairs, and commercial use also void the warranty.
